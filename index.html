Welcome to Ad Link Media

The first agency to reach all of UP from one desk

Ad Link was established in 2004 as the first complete resource to reach the whole of Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Rajasthan, with a then-new concept in outdoor advertising: mobile hoarding.

Today Ad Link Media Pvt. Ltd. adds road shows, satellite and radio channels, ad films, documentaries and complete outdoor and indoor advertising. Art directors, creative directors, marketing managers and publishers rely on us for high-quality advertising.

Questions

Before you book

What is mobile hoarding advertising?

A mobile hoarding is a lit or flex billboard mounted on a vehicle that drives a planned route through markets, highways and towns, so your ad reaches people where static hoardings are not allowed or not visible.

Which areas does Ad Link cover?

We run campaigns across urban and rural Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand and Rajasthan from our Lucknow office, and can extend to other North Indian states on request.

Can you handle a campaign end to end?

Yes. We plan the media, develop the concept, produce creatives and ad films, run the campaign on ground, TV, radio and digital, and share photo reports.

Do you work with small budgets?

Yes. We build packages for local shops and single-town launches as well as for national brands running multi-state campaigns.

Ready to be seen across North India?

Share your target towns and dates. We'll send a media plan and an itemised quote within one working day.
